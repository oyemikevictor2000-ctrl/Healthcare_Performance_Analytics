# 🏥 Healthcare Performance & Executive Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Executive Summary
This Power BI executive dashboard delivers an end-to-end performance analysis of multi-branch hospital operations across Nigeria (covering **Rivers, Lagos, Abuja, and Anambra** states). By unifying complex clinical, operational, and financial datasets, the dashboard provides hospital administrators and executive decision-makers with real-time visibility into revenue generation, patient care throughput, operational bottlenecks, and department-level profitability.

---

## 🛠️ Data Architecture & ETL Pipeline

* **Microsoft Excel:** Applied initial data structuring, clean formatting, and validation protocols on raw healthcare transaction logs and patient records.
* **Power Query:** Performed complete ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) operations, including data type alignment, missing value remediation, custom column calculations, and relational modeling across state branches.
* **Power BI Desktop:** Developed a multi-page dynamic report leveraging advanced DAX modeling, custom measures, bookmark navigation, tooltips, and interactive filtering.

## 💡 Comprehensive Business & Operational Insights

### 1. Financial & Revenue Performance
* **Regional Leaders:** **Rivers State** ranks as the top-performing territory, generating **~₦12.7M** in revenue. Conversely, **Anambra State** represents the lowest-performing branch at **₦11.33M**, indicating a key target for regional growth strategy.
* **Department Profitability:** **Pediatrics** emerged as the primary profit engine, yielding **~₦3.7M** in profit, followed by Outpatient (**₦3.14M**) and Emergency (**₦2.93M**). **Maternity** generated the lowest total profit at **~₦2.8M**.
* **Financial Gap:** Total revenue sits significantly below the ₦200M target threshold, driven by negative revenue variances across all main operating branches (e.g., Port Harcourt at -₦196.1M variance).

### 2. Operational Efficiency & Capacity Bottlenecks
* **Department Volume:** **Pediatrics** is the busiest department with **515 patient visits**, followed closely by Outpatient and Pharmacy.
* **Wait Time Delays:** The **Laboratory** department recorded the longest average waiting time at **47.0 minutes**, closely followed by Pediatrics and Pharmacy. High processing delays in Emergency and Outpatient departments create systemic bottlenecks for overall patient flow.
* **Patient Outcomes:** Over half of all recorded patients (**54.08% / 649 patients**) were successfully **Recovered**. Admitted patients accounted for **21.92% (263)**, Referred made up **12.92% (155)**, and Follow-up represented **11.08% (133)**.

### 3. Patient Experience & Satisfaction Scores
* **Departmental Satisfaction:** **Pharmacy** achieved the highest overall satisfaction average at **3.74 / 5.0**, while **Maternity** recorded the lowest at **3.69 / 5.0**.
* **Branch Breakdown:** High-volume branches such as Port Harcourt, Trans Amadi, and Obio-Akpor show consistent wait time metrics averaging between 43 to 49 minutes per patient visit.

## 📑 Dashboard Layout & Page Navigation

1. **Healthcare Performance Executive Dashboard:** High-level executive overview tracking overall financials, patient throughput trends, state-by-state breakdowns, and real-time executive summaries.
2. **Hospital Operations & Operational Efficiency:** Deep-dive page examining busiest/longest-wait departments, outcome distribution, branch-level patient counts, and operational throughput metrics.
3. **Hospital Financial Performance:** Financial breakdown detailing departmental profit margin percentages (e.g., Lagos at 38.9%), MoM revenue growth (7.6%), and target variance tracking.
4. **Patient Experience:** Focused analysis on wait times versus satisfaction scores across services, branches, and age grades (Elderly, Adult, Teenager, Child).
